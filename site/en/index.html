Features

✅

Tasks

One-off, daily and weekly tasks with priority, notes and reminder time; drag to reorder, swipe to complete or delete.

📅

Weekly tasks

Choose the first day of the week and "remind until done" modes.

📊

History & stats

A completion log that survives task deletion, streaks and a 7-day chart.

🎯

Yearly goals

Milestone or numeric goals, with progress vs. the elapsed year.

📱

Screen time

Screen-on time, unlocks, per-app usage and daily limits — the data never leaves your device.

🔔

Reminders

On-time notifications with a "Done ✓" action, follow-ups and an evening "plan tomorrow" reminder.

🧩

Home-screen widgets

A task list and a summary widget.

🎨

Your look

9 color themes, light / dark / AMOLED, backgrounds, and backup / restore to a file.

Privacy

  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • All your data stays on your phone, and the app works offline.
  • Screen-time data never leaves your device.
  • Backup and restore to a JSON file you keep.

FAQ

Is Mongez free?

Yes — free and open source (GPL-3.0), with no ads.

Does it need the internet or an account?

No. It works offline with no account; all your data stays on your phone.

Does it support Arabic?

Yes — Arabic and English with full right-to-left support.

Which phones?

Android 7 or newer.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Settings → Data → Save a backup, then restore it on the new phone.
