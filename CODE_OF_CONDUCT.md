# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Mongez to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience level,
background, identity, language or beliefs. We pledge to make taking part in this community a
harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project.
- Respect different viewpoints and both languages used here (Arabic and English).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that don't follow this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently ban anyone
whose behavior they consider harmful.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through GitHub
([@AdamG00D](https://github.com/AdamG00D)). All reports will be handled confidentially.
