Mongez (مُنجز) is a simple planner in Arabic and English: write your tasks, follow your yearly goals, and see where your phone time goes. It works fully offline. Free, open source, no ads, no account.

The app follows your phone's language (Arabic or English), and you can switch it in Settings.

TASKS
• One-off, daily and weekly tasks with priority, notes and a reminder time
• Drag to reorder, or sort by time or priority
• Swipe to complete or delete, with undo
• Postpone, copy to tomorrow, carry unfinished tasks over
• Weekly tasks on chosen days or "once any day", with a configurable first day of the week

REMINDERS
• A reminder at the time you set, with a "Done" button on the notification
• Follow-up reminders for unfinished tasks
• An evening reminder to plan tomorrow

HISTORY AND STATS
• A permanent completion log that survives deleting the task
• Per-type counters and day streaks
• A 7-day chart and your most completed tasks

YEARLY GOALS
• Numeric goals (read 12 books) or milestone goals
• Your progress compared with how much of the year has passed

SCREEN TIME (OPTIONAL)
• Daily and weekly screen-on time and unlock count
• Time spent in each app
• Daily limits for the apps you choose to track
This feature needs the "Usage access" permission, which you grant yourself in the phone's settings. The data is only shown to you and stays on your device.

LOOK AND FEEL
• 9 color themes, light, dark and AMOLED black
• Built-in backgrounds or your own photo
• A small celebration when you finish the day's tasks
• Home-screen widgets for today's tasks and a summary

PRIVACY
• No account, no sign-up
• No ads, no trackers
• The app does not even have the Internet permission
• Backup and restore through a file you keep
